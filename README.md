# Kumora

**Many Threads. One Vision.**

Kumora is an AI-powered construction and business management platform designed to bring projects, workforce, finances, and business records together in one place.

## 🎯 Project Vision

To simplify construction business management through organized digital records, regional voice input, automated calculations, and clear financial reports.

## ✨ Planned Features

- **Organization Management** — Manage organizations, teams, and permissions.
- **Projects & Construction Sites** — Track clients, project timelines, progress, and site records.
- **Workforce Management** — Manage workers, site assignments, and daily attendance.
- **Payroll & Payments** — Track wages, advances, payments, and outstanding balances.
- **Personal & Business Finance** — Record daily expenses, income, and financial transactions.
- **AI Voice Assistant** — Convert regional-language voice notes into structured, readable records.
- **Bill Converter** — Convert handwritten bills into editable Excel files and PDF documents.
- **Reports & Analytics** — Generate date-range, project-wise, and financial reports.
- **Labour Portal** — Provide workers with read-only access to their attendance, earnings, and payments.
- **Feedback & Disputes** — Allow workers to report attendance or payment discrepancies.

## 🏗️ Planned Technology Stack

- Frontend: React, TypeScript, and Vite
- Backend: Django REST Framework
- Database: PostgreSQL
- Real-time Updates: Django Channels
- Background Processing: Celery and Redis
- Document Generation: OpenPyXL and ReportLab
- AI Services: Regional speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and handwriting recognition

*These technologies are planned and have not necessarily been implemented.*

## 🚧 Current Status

**Project Stage: Planning and Initial Setup**

Kumora is currently in the initial planning stage. Features will be implemented incrementally, starting with the project foundation and user interface.

## 🔐 Security & Privacy

Kumora aims to protect sensitive business and financial information through authentication, role-based permissions, and secure record management.

## 📌 Development Approach

The project will be developed in small, organized steps. Each feature will be designed, implemented, tested, and documented before moving to the next stage.
