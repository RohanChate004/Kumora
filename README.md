# Kumora

**Many Threads. One Vision.**

Kumora is an AI-powered construction and business management platform designed to bring projects, workforce, finances, and business records together in one place.

## 🌐 Why the Name Kumora?

The name **Kumora** is inspired by the Japanese word *kumo* (蜘蛛), meaning **spider**, combined with a smooth, modern-sounding ending to create a distinctive brand name.

A spider connects many individual threads to form one unified web. Similarly, Kumora connects multiple parts of a business — construction sites, workers, attendance, payroll, expenses, clients, documents, and financial reports — into one organized platform.

Each feature represents a different thread, while Kumora brings them together into a single, connected system.

The name represents our core philosophy:

- **Connection** — Bring projects, people, and financial records together.
- **Organization** — Turn scattered information into structured, accessible records.
- **Intelligence** — Use AI and regional voice technology to simplify everyday work.
- **Clarity** — Help users understand their business, expenses, and outstanding payments.
- **Growth** — Build a flexible platform that can grow alongside a business.

### Our Tagline

**Many Threads. One Vision.**

Kumora aims to make complex business management feel simple by connecting every important detail in one place.
